Zneoba (Morality)

In this track, I explored the idea of building tension from a state of low energy and gradually directing it toward someone or somewhere. During live performances and DJ sets, I am drawn to creating this kind of tension — building it slowly, letting it go, asking a question and allowing the music to provide the response, waiting for a conclusion and then reflecting on it. Technically, I started from a simple idea and developed it into an evolving sound. I used pads, textural and atmospheric elements, combined with repetitive rhythmic patterns, to create a sense of continuous movement and gradual transformation. This approach is also connected to the use of repetitive rhythms and evolving sounds in ancient rituals, where sustained repetition was used to enter altered states of consciousness, reach a sense of Nirvana, and explore something within oneself. For me, the track follows a similar process: repetition creates the space, evolution creates the movement, and tension becomes a way of reaching somewhere beyond the initial idea.
