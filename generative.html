ხვრელი (Khvreli) - Hole

I think every person has something deep inside that needs to be released. Sometimes it comes out through actions, sometimes through words and etc.. Over time, people forget how connected the soul is to everything we do — our decisions, emotions, movements, actions and the way we express ourselves. For me, music becomes a way to reconnect with that part, to feel alive, to feel that im here in that moment. While making this track, I tried not to overthink anything. I just followed the feeling and let the process guide me naturally. I think that is one of the purest states for creating music - when you stop overthinking every detail and simply feel what is happening in the moment. The technical foundation of the track comes from self-generative sound design. The rolling, falling click texture is built from a generative patch that I created and later sampled. I usually prefer having control over movement and progression in my tracks, so instead of leaving the patch fully random, I recorded and reshaped it into a stable texture. It works more like a moving layer inside the track — never demanding attention as the main element, but constantly existing in the background and giving the piece its pulse and atmosphere.
