Makhinji

"Makhinji" in georgian language means UGLY Sometimes, when something does not fit the standard idea of taste, people tend not to like it. But since childhood, I have always been drawn to things and people that are unconventional — things that some might even consider ugly. While creating this track, I knew the sound was not clean or pure, as it is shaped by heavy distortion, but I chose to keep it raw exactly as it is. In this piece of music, nothing is classical or traditional — neither the sound nor the arrangement. It is simply a live jam, captured in the moment, which some might even consider an ugly piece of music. I wanted to emphasize that everything has something worth noticing, something that asks to be understood and appreciated.
