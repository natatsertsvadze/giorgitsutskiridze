Priad davikarge (Totally lost)

This track has a very deep meaning in my life. Before making it, I was mainly focused on drum patterns, and melodic sequences. Of course, I was always interested in atmospheric and textural sounds, but I mostly used them as a background layer to support and fulfill music. With this track, I decided to approach music differently and fully rely on atmospheric sound — no beats, no drums, no melody. Just me, you, and a flowing state. Technically, I started with my own voice and used VCV Rack to create an electroacoustic patch, processing my voice through different effects. I then sampled the result and brought it into Ableton, where I built a buzzing, evolving sub layer. On top of that, I added atmospheric pads, and finally, I did backgroud sounds, FXs, arrangement. The track became an exploration of how much can be expressed through texture, space, and movement alone, without relying on rhythmic or melodic structures.
